Getting Started

First Time Setup

  1. Download the App: Available on iOS App Store and Google Play Store
  2. Account Setup: Contact your administrator for login credentials
  3. Permissions: Grant necessary permissions for microphone (voice notes) and location (if enabled)
  4. Initial Sync: The app will sync with your company's data automatically

Quick Start Guide

  • Start Tracking: Tap the "Start Timer" button to begin time tracking
  • Add Notes: Use the microphone button for voice-to-text notes
  • Link Jobs: Select job assignments from your assigned work orders
  • Generate Summaries: Use AI to create professional job reports

Features & How-To

Time Tracking

Starting a Timer

  • Tap the "Start Timer" button on the main screen
  • Select a job assignment if prompted
  • Your status will change to "Active"

Stopping a Timer

  • Tap the "Stop Timer" button
  • Add any final notes or job details
  • Your status will return to "Idle"

Status Types

  • Active: Currently working on a job
  • On Lunch: Taking a break
  • Driving: Traveling between jobs
  • Idle: Not currently working

Job Management

Creating Work Orders

  • Tap "New Job" from the main menu
  • Fill in customer information and job details
  • Assign to specific technicians if needed

Adding Job Notes

  • Use the microphone for voice-to-text
  • Type notes manually in the text field
  • Include relevant details about work performed

AI Summary Generation

  • Tap "Generate Summary" after adding notes
  • Review the AI-generated report
  • Edit if needed before saving

Voice-to-Text

Using Voice Notes

  • Tap the microphone icon
  • Speak clearly and at a normal pace
  • Tap again to stop recording
  • Review and edit the transcribed text

Tips for Better Transcription

  • Speak in a quiet environment
  • Use clear, professional language
  • Include technical details and measurements
  • Mention any issues or follow-up needed

Troubleshooting

Common Issues

App Won't Start

  • Check your internet connection
  • Restart the app completely
  • Ensure you have the latest version
  • Contact support if the issue persists

Can't Log In

  • Verify your username and password
  • Check that your account is active
  • Ensure you have internet connectivity
  • Contact your administrator for account issues

Timer Not Working

  • Check that you're logged in
  • Verify your internet connection
  • Try stopping and restarting the timer
  • Restart the app if needed

Voice Notes Not Working

  • Grant microphone permissions in device settings
  • Check that your device's microphone is working
  • Ensure you're in a quiet environment
  • Try typing notes manually as an alternative

Data Not Syncing

  • Check your internet connection
  • Pull down to refresh the app
  • Log out and log back in
  • Contact support if sync issues persist

Performance Issues

App Running Slowly

  • Close other apps to free up memory
  • Restart your device
  • Check available storage space
  • Update to the latest app version

Battery Drain

  • Close the app when not in use
  • Reduce screen brightness
  • Disable unnecessary background processes
  • Check for app updates

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use the app offline?
A: Yes, the app works offline and will sync when you reconnect to the internet.

Q: How do I change my password?
A: Contact your administrator to change your password.

Q: Can I track time for multiple jobs at once?
A: No, you can only track time for one job at a time. Stop the current timer before starting a new one.

Q: How accurate is the voice-to-text feature?
A: Voice recognition is generally accurate in quiet environments. Always review and edit transcribed text.

Q: Can I export my time data?
A: Yes, time data can be exported through the web dashboard by your administrator.

Q: What happens if I forget to stop the timer?
A: The timer will continue running. You can stop it later and adjust the end time if needed.

Q: Can I edit time entries after they're saved?
A: Yes, you can edit time entries within a reasonable time period. Contact your administrator for older entries.

Q: How secure is my data?
A: All data is encrypted and transmitted securely. We follow industry best practices for data protection.
